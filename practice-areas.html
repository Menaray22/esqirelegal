Esquires’ Legal is a strategically positioned Nigerian Maritime and Admiralty practice, providing specialised legal services to international shipowners, charterers, marine insurers, P&I Clubs, offshore operators and global law firms requiring Nigerian maritime enforcement capability.

Operating within one of Africa’s most commercially active maritime jurisdictions, we serve as trusted Nigerian counsel for cross-border maritime disputes, vessel arrest, offshore energy operations and shipping-related transactions.

⚓ Core Maritime Capabilities

Ship Arrest & Emergency Maritime Enforcement
We provide rapid-response legal services for the arrest and release of vessels within Nigerian territorial waters and ports. Our team is experienced in initiating and defending in rem proceedings before the Federal High Court, securing maritime claims efficiently and negotiating acceptable security (including P&I Club Letters of Undertaking and bank guarantees).

Admiralty Litigation & Dispute Resolution
We represent clients in complex maritime disputes including:

  • Cargo loss and damage claims
  • Charterparty disputes (time and voyage charters)
  • Collision and salvage claims
  • Maritime liens and statutory claims
  • Marine insurance disputes

Marine Insurance & P&I Club Representation
We act for and collaborate with Protection & Indemnity (P&I) Clubs, marine insurers and their correspondents, defending and prosecuting claims involving cargo liabilities, pollution, personal injury and indemnity exposures.

Offshore Energy & Marine Logistics
Leveraging our strong Oil & Gas practice, we advise on:

  • Offshore vessel contracts (FPSOs, FSOs, supply vessels)
  • Marine logistics and support services
  • Regulatory compliance within Nigeria’s offshore energy framework

Maritime Arbitration & International Trade
We provide representation and support in maritime arbitrations, including matters governed by:

  • LMAA (London Maritime Arbitrators Association)
  • ICC Arbitration
  • Ad hoc proceedings

We also advise on disputes arising from Bills of Lading, FOB/CIF contracts, Incoterms and international trade frameworks.

Our Maritime Philosophy – “Ripples in Practice”

At Esquires’ Legal, our approach to Admiralty Law reflects a deeper jurisprudential philosophy — that every maritime dispute, enforcement action or advisory mandate creates a ripple effect across commerce, law and global trade.

As expressed in our Principal’s published work “Ripples… An Ode to Admiralty”, we recognise that Maritime Law operates in ever-expanding circles of impact — from vessel to cargo, from port to jurisdiction, from dispute to precedent.

We therefore approach each instruction not merely as a legal task, but as a strategic intervention within the global maritime ecosystem.

  • Jurisdictional Strength: Nigeria as a high-value maritime enforcement jurisdiction
  • Rapid Execution: Ability to act swiftly in vessel arrest situations
  • International Coordination: Seamless collaboration with foreign counsel
  • Port Reach: Operational familiarity with major Nigerian ports (Apapa, Tin Can, Onne, Warri, etc.)
  • Commercial Awareness: Deep understanding of shipping, insurance and offshore industry dynamics
