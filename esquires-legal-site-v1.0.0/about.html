About Esquires’ Legal

“Integrity in Efficacy”

Mission, Vision and Purpose

Who We Are
To deliver justifiably, unique and exemplary legal and advisory service.

Our Mission

To satisfy Clients with uncompromising professional legal services including other areas of enterprise and endeavour related to but outside the legal framework.

Our Vision

To provide superior bespoke legal services and advisory solutions globally that meet unique challenges and exceed stated expectations in accordance with the highest professional standards and ethics.

How We Work

Our Strategy…

To assist clients, we draw from a wide pool of expertise and resources to deliver specialised and personalised service. We believe that what distinguishes us from other law firms is that we always ensure that our advice is practical, yet innovative and that the work is performed on time on budget, with the client in mind.

We carry out our clients' instructions with minimum delay and are committed to committing a Law Firm based on expertise and services, with a personal touch.

To provide excellent career opportunities and training for Counsel in our Legal Team and staffers in our Administrative Teams to ensure optimum value to our clients, who are usually stakeholders in their various industries, Esquires' legal remains a proactive and progressive contributor to the development and sustenance of the Nigerian Legal System and Jurisprudence.

The Firm’s ethos is to ensure that the ordinary person receives just as good quality legal advice as our state bodies, big organisations and multi-nationals. Esquires' legal is unwavering in its commitment to providing first class legal professional service.

Our Promise

Our Commitment…

Using all forms of information technology related tools, including modern data processing equipment to improve efficiency and currency, we are dedicated to the advancement of law and research. Each client will be attended to closely by a partner /associate who is responsible for the management of the overall relationship. We adopt a “hands-on” and “people-orientated” approach whereby clients’ are treated as individuals by firm partners/associates and have easy access to all members of their specialist team. strive to build trust and bring hard work to every case and project. We have built a solid reputation amongst our clients by outlining specific expectations during the earliest discussions getting to know our client and meeting and/or surpassing their expectations. We are qualified to accommodate all aspects of your claims and defence/project or particular brief.

What We Deliver

Our Objectives…

We ensure that we remain focused on our professional target which is to satisfy our clients. Our goal is helping you reach your goal, offering insightful knowledge, creative thinking and maximum value to all our clients, all of the time.

At the end of conclusion of each brief, our clients are assured that they received exceptional professional legal service because we work to understand each client’s particular needs and deliver services and specialty management solutions that will add value to their lives and businesses.
