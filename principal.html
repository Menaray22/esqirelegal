Principal Counsel

Austin J. Otah

Esq, Notary, MA, BL  ·  Called to Bar, Nigeria [1990]  ·  MA Law, [LGU] now Lond. Met. Univ.

Principal Consulting Counsel / Solicitor / Barrister

Biography and Credentials

Background & Education

Austin is a dually qualified legal practitioner Chief Consulting Officer of Esquires’ Legal, an international based legal consultancy Firm and Set of Chambers; an Associate of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators, Member of the Nigerian Bar Association, the African Bar Association, International Bar Association based in Nigeria.

He attended University of Ilorin, Ilorin, Kwara State and Obafemi Awolowo University [formerly University of Ife], the latter from which he graduated with honours, in 1989. He was called to the Nigerian Bar in 1990. He graduated with honours with a Master’s Degree at Law from the London Guildhall University, London (now London Metropolitan University), UK, with a bias in international trade, banking and commerce and international arbitration.

Practice Focus

Austin currently works in a consultancy capacity both domestically and internationally as the Principal Consulting Counsel & Solicitor of Messrs Esquires’ Legal, an International Legal Practice & Consultancy Firm. He is an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (CIARB) and occupies the Office of a Notary Public. His core areas of Practice include legal and legislative drafting, company secretarial services, mediation/negotiations/ADR, General Litigation, Law of the Internet, immigration law, conveyancing & Real Estate, consumer protection/competition & antitrust law, Labour Law and Trade Unions; and international commercial law. He has developed a specialised focus on Maritime and Admiralty Law, advising on vessel-related disputes, maritime enforcement, marine insurance and international trade matters. He has been involved in:

  • Ship arrest and maritime claims strategy
  • Advisory and dispute resolution in cargo and charterparty matters
  • Representation linked to P&I Club instructions
  • Maritime arbitration and international commercial disputes

He is also the author of the poem: “Ripples… An Ode to Admiralty” — a published work reflecting the evolution, philosophy and dynamic nature of maritime jurisprudence, demonstrating a unique blend of legal intellect and thought leadership within the Admiralty space.

Media, Entertainment & Sports

He also has advised on matters within the Media, Entertainment and Sports sectors, with particular experience in sports dispute resolution and international arbitration.

He was part of the legal team representing Samson Siasia before the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS), demonstrating exposure to high-level international sports adjudication, in which his life ban and significant fine was overturned.

His work spans:

  • Sports disciplinary and regulatory defence
  • Contractual disputes in sports and entertainment
  • Advisory on commercial and media-related transactions

He brings a strategic and reputation-conscious approach to matters involving public figures and institutions.

Family & Customary Law

Wearing two hats, having experienced the legal systems of both Nigeria and UK, in the area of Family/Customary legal issues as it affects Nigerians both home and abroad since the 1990s. He has also written Legal Opinions in this area of Law and has handled various aspects of statutory family and customary Law issues in property and family matters over the years in the UK, Nigerian and generally in Commonwealth jurisdictions. These include issues concerning children rights and family adoptions. Recently he gave expert testimony on the Nigerian Legal Marriage System before a Canadian Superior Court of Record and is officially judicially recognised as an expert on the subject-matter.

Beyond the Law

Austin is an avid reader and writer, composes poetry, loves travelling, photography, scrabble, chess, football and keeping fit. He is also a radio analyst on Silverbird’s Rhythm 94-7 FM and Nigeriainfo.com FM, WE FM and appears as a guest on Television [NTA, AIT, Silverbird, Liberty and People’s TV] in Abuja, Nigeria, dealing with significant sporting events, current affairs and legal issues in Nigeria and around the world. He is a contributor to several published works and co-authored a recent book: ‘How to Write a Will in Nigeria: A Layman’s Guide.’ Austin is also on the Roster of Counsel for the African Court on Human and Peoples’ Rights [see www.african-court.org].

Current Roles

He is presently the CEO, Legum Consult Limited, a legislative and public affairs Consultancy founded by reputable and experienced former and current Legislators, Advocates and Academics in the field of Law, Legislation, lobbyist and allied areas. He is actively involved in his local Nigerian Bar Association Branch and is the Chairman, Legislative Advocacy and Law Reform Committee amongst other roles.
